About Kangen Water

What exactly is Kangen Water?

“Kangen” is a Japanese word meaning “return to origin.” Kangen Water® is filtered tap water that has been run through Enagic’s LeveLuk ionizers — a process that restructures ordinary water into ionized, alkaline, hydrogen-rich water designed for daily drinking and dozens of household uses.

How It Works

From tap water to seven types of water

The LeveLuk machine sits on your countertop and connects to your faucet. Inside, two things happen.

1

Filtration

Your tap water first passes through a multi-stage filter that reduces chlorine, sediment and other unwanted particles.

2

Electrolysis

The filtered water flows over medical-grade platinum-coated titanium plates. Electrolysis separates it into alkaline and acidic streams.

3

Select Your Water

Choose a setting on the touchscreen — drinking, beauty, clean or strong — and the machine pours the right water for the task.

The Basics

Three words you’ll hear a lot

⚖

Alkaline

Drinking water from the machine typically sits around pH 8.5–9.5, on the alkaline side of the pH scale rather than acidic.

🔬

Micro-clustered

Advocates describe the restructured water as forming smaller molecular clusters, which they say makes it feel lighter and easier to drink.

⚡

Hydrogen-rich

The electrolysis process produces water rich in molecular hydrogen, often talked about for its antioxidant properties.
