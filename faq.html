Ask a Question What is a Kangen Water Machine?
What is Kangen Water?
Kangen Water® is filtered tap water that has been ionized by an Enagic LeveLuk machine. The process produces alkaline, hydrogen-rich, micro-clustered water for drinking, plus a range of other waters for beauty, cooking and cleaning.
What is a Kangen Water machine?
A Kangen Water machine is an Enagic LeveLuk water ionizer. It connects to your kitchen faucet, filters the water, and then uses electrolysis to produce several different types of water — from alkaline drinking water to strong acidic cleaning water.
How much does a Kangen Water machine cost?
The LeveLuk K8 is $5,890 before tax. Other LeveLuk models cost less — the right one depends on your home and budget. Message me and I’ll share current pricing and payment options.
Which Enagic Kangen Water machine is right for me?
It depends on your household size, how you plan to use the water, and your budget. The LeveLuk K8 is a popular all-round choice, but there are other models too. I’m happy to walk you through the differences so you can make an informed decision.
How does the LeveLuk K8 work?
The K8 filters your tap water, then passes it over eight platinum-coated titanium electrode plates. Electrolysis separates the water into alkaline and acidic streams, and you pick the water type you want on the touchscreen.
Where can I buy a Kangen Water machine?

How many types of water does one machine make?
Five kinds of water, across seven pH settings — from strong acidic (pH 2.5) for cleaning, through beauty water (pH 6.0) and clean water (pH 7.0), to Kangen drinking water at three strengths (pH 8.5, 9.0 and 9.5) and strong Kangen water (pH 11.5) for tough kitchen jobs. See all five explained →
Does the water change when it’s heated?
Yes. When Kangen water is heated, its ORP (oxidation-reduction potential) and pH values will change. For drinking, many people enjoy it cool and fresh.
What about when it’s refrigerated or chilled?
When chilled, the ORP will change, but the pH value can remain stable for up to about a week depending on the quality of the water and how it’s stored.
How long will the pH stay stable?
Properly refrigerated in a sealed container, the pH can remain stable for up to around 7 days, depending on water quality and storage conditions. At room temperature, ORP and pH gradually change over time, so it’s best to refrigerate or drink within a few days.
Can I use it for baby formula or with medicine?
The neutral pH 7.0 “clean” setting produces pure, balanced water that is commonly used for mixing baby formula and for taking with medication. As always, follow your pediatrician’s or doctor’s guidance.
Is it safe for pets and plants?
Yes — the alkaline drinking water is popular for pets, and beauty water is used to help revive cut flowers and houseplants.
